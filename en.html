ABOUT YOSUGA

TEAM YOSUGA is a community-driven racket sports club based in Northern Mie, Japan.
We welcome players of all ages and skill levels through events, practice sessions, and social activities focused on Padel, Pickleball, Typti, and Tennis.
Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced player, you'll find a friendly and supportive environment here.

OUR SPORTS

Padel

A unique racket sport that uses glass walls. Easy to enjoy long rallies and perfect for friends and families.

Pickleball

One of the fastest-growing sports in the United States. Easy to learn and fun for players of all ages.

Typti

An exciting new racket sport from the United States that combines fast rallies, simple rules, and fun for all generations.

Tennis

The classic racket sport. From junior beginner lessons to adult practice sessions, everyone can enjoy the game.

FAQ

I'm new to racket sports. Can I still join?

Absolutely. Many participants start with no prior experience. Our staff will gladly help you learn the rules and basic techniques.

Can I participate on my own?

Of course. Many participants join alone and quickly become part of the community.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. Rental rackets and paddles are available. Just bring comfortable sportswear and shoes.

Can children participate?

Yes. Please contact us via Instagram DM for details regarding age requirements and participation.

Do I need a reservation?

Yes. First-time participants are asked to register through our application form or Instagram DM.

Are there any extra fees?

No additional fees are required. Rental equipment is included in the participation fee.

MESSAGE

Kyohei Fukuta

Founder / Representative

I started TEAM YOSUGA because I wanted more people in Northern Mie to experience the joy of picking up a racket, meeting new friends, and discovering new opportunities through sports.

Padel, Pickleball, Typti and Tennis are all simple to start, yet surprisingly addictive and enjoyable.

The smile after hitting a great shot and the conversations that naturally begin with people you've just met are what make sports special.

We believe these experiences help children grow and provide adults with new friendships and excitement.

Beginners and families are always welcome. Even if you've never held a racket before, we'd love to see you at one of our events.
